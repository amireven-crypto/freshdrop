Delivery time

Approx. 7–15 business days after payment. To be honest: our goods ship directly from partner warehouses, some of them outside the EU. That takes longer than with the big online retailers – but it keeps our prices fair. Once real orders are enabled, we plan to send you a tracking number by email as soon as your parcel is on its way (not available in the demo).

Customs & taxes

All prices include VAT. For parcels from outside the EU, a customs/handling fee of around €3 per item may apply under the EU rules for low-value parcels; this is not a promise and the exact amount depends on the origin of the goods and the rules in force at the time of the order. {{Decide before launch: whether this fee is included in the price, shown at checkout, or paid by the customer on delivery – and IOSS registration}}

Returns

You have a 14-day right of withdrawal from receipt of the goods. Here is how it works:

  1. Send a short message to pixelpopsite@gmail.com or use the withdrawal function – include your order number.
  2. You will receive the return address: {{return address in the EU}}.
  3. Send the goods back within 14 days of your message.
  4. Refund within 14 days via your original payment method.

Damaged or wrong item?

Send us a photo at pixelpopsite@gmail.com soon after delivery – we will arrange a replacement or refund. Your statutory warranty rights remain unaffected.
